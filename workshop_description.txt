[h1]Qud HUD[/h1]
A heads-up display for your second monitor. Glance over and see everything that needs your attention, without digging through sub screens.

[h2]What it shows[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Pressing matters:[/b] critical problems first. Low HP, diseases, incapacitating effects, thirst, hunger, burning or freezing, adjacent and dangerous hostiles, broken gear, empty energy cells.
[*]Effects with durations and details
[*]Ability cooldowns, soonest ready first
[*]Hostiles in sight with HP, level and difficulty rating
[*]Attributes, AV, DV, MA, quickness, move speed and resistances
[*]Thirst, hunger, fresh water, temperature and carry weight
[/list]

[h2]How to use[/h2]
Load a game. The message log tells you where the display page was written (normally Documents/QudHUD/hud.html). Open it in any browser on your second monitor. It updates right before each of your turns.

Drag panels by their ≡ grip to rearrange them. Your layout is saved.

[h2]Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*]No server or internet connection needed; the page reads a local file.
[*]Keep the mod enabled for saves made with it.
[*]Source and issues: GITHUB LINK
[/list]
